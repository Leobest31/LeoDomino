Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-09

App: LeoDomino (Version 1) · App ID: com.leodomino.app · Operator: LEONORD B PHILOSTIN · Contact: support@leodomino.com · Website: https://leodomino.com · This policy: https://leodomino.com/privacy

This Privacy Policy applies to LeoDomino Version 1 (V1) distributed through Google Play, the Apple App Store, and any official LeoDomino web or PWA distribution.

1. Overview

LeoDomino V1 is an offline-first domino game.

In Version 1:

  • You do not need a LeoDomino account.
  • There is no LeoDomino online multiplayer, League, friends list, or chat.
  • Gameplay does not require signing into a LeoDomino cloud service.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.

This policy describes how LeoDomino V1 handles information in the released app. Future online features are not part of V1 and are not treated as already active here.

2. Information LeoDomino V1 does not collect

LeoDomino V1 does not:

  • Require or create a LeoDomino user account
  • Collect a name, email address, phone number, or payment card through the app for gameplay
  • Use LeoDomino advertising networks or in-app ad tracking
  • Use LeoDomino analytics or usage-tracking services
  • Use LeoDomino crash-reporting services that upload gameplay data to us
  • Offer LeoDomino cloud save or LeoDomino-operated game servers in V1
  • Support social login
  • Let users upload photos, videos, files, or shared links inside the game

If you never contact us, LeoDomino V1 does not receive personal contact details from you through the app.

3. Information stored on your device

LeoDomino V1 may store information locally on your device, including:

  • An in-progress match (so you can resume)
  • Preferences such as language, sound, theme, tile look, and vibration
  • Setup choices such as player count, AI difficulty, and game style
  • Optional local career statistics (for example matches played or wins on that device)

This information stays on your device unless you back it up, share it, or transfer it yourself using your operating system or another tool.

LeoDomino V1 may also use ordinary on-device features such as:

  • Local storage
  • Vibration (if enabled and supported)
  • Game sound (if enabled)

4. Internet access

Some app packages may include a general internet permission because of how the app is packaged.

LeoDomino V1 is designed so you can play offline. Version 1 does not use a LeoDomino account service, multiplayer service, analytics service, or cloud sync service for gameplay.

If you install LeoDomino as a web app / PWA, your browser may download and keep app files for offline use. That is for running the app, not for uploading your match history to LeoDomino servers.

5. App stores, your device, and the operating system

Google, Apple, your device maker, and your operating system may process information under their own privacy policies. Examples can include app install records, store purchases, crash reports you share with the platform, or identifiers used by the store.

LeoDomino does not control those platform practices. Please review:

  • Google Play / Google privacy information (Android)
  • Apple App Store / Apple privacy information (iOS)
  • Your device and system privacy settings

This policy does not claim that Google, Apple, or device platforms collect no data.

6. If you contact us

If you email support@leodomino.com, we receive whatever you include in your message (such as your email address and a description of the issue).

We use that information to respond and provide support, unless the law requires otherwise.

7. How local information is used

On-device data is used to:

  • Save and resume matches
  • Remember your language and settings
  • Apply the game style you chose
  • Keep optional local statistics on that device
  • Respect sound and vibration preferences

LeoDomino V1 does not use your local game data to show targeted ads inside the app.

8. Sharing

LeoDomino V1 does not sell your personal information.

Because Version 1 does not run a LeoDomino account cloud or LeoDomino analytics pipeline for the app, the app does not share your match history or settings with LeoDomino servers.

If you contact us by email, we may disclose that correspondence when required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect rights and safety—only as reasonably necessary.

9. Children

LeoDomino V1 is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Version 1 has no account system, so we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a LeoDomino account.

If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, contact support@leodomino.com. We will take reasonable steps to delete that correspondence where appropriate.

Parents and guardians should supervise device use and store account settings.

10. No accounts — how to delete local data

LeoDomino V1 has no user accounts.

That means:

  • There is no LeoDomino account to delete
  • There is no LeoDomino cloud profile to erase through an in-app account page

To remove LeoDomino’s local data from your device, you can:

  1. Clear the app’s storage or cache in your device settings, and/or
  2. Uninstall the app, and/or
  3. For a browser/PWA install, clear site data for LeoDomino in the browser settings

Important: Clearing storage or reinstalling usually deletes saved matches, local statistics, and preferences on that device. LeoDomino cannot restore that data in V1 because it was not stored on LeoDomino servers.

To request deletion of an email you sent us, contact support@leodomino.com.

11. Retention

  • On your device: Local data remains until you clear it, reset the app, or uninstall.
  • Email: Kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal or recordkeeping needs of LEONORD B PHILOSTIN.

12. Security

We design Version 1 to keep gameplay data on your device. No electronic storage is perfectly secure. Use a device passcode or biometrics and keep your system updated. If your device is lost and you have no separate backup, LeoDomino cannot recover local saves.

13. Languages

The app may be available in Haitian Creole, English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese. Your language choice is stored on your device. This Privacy Policy is provided in English; the operator may publish translations later.

14. Future features

Online multiplayer, League, rankings, championships, friends, or chat may arrive in a later version. They are not active in V1.

If a future version adds accounts, online play, analytics, or cloud services, this Privacy Policy will be updated when those features ship (or before), with a new effective date.

15. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. When a new version is published at https://leodomino.com/privacy, the effective date above will change. Continued use after that date means you acknowledge the updated policy, except where the law requires additional consent.
